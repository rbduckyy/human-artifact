AI: What Are We Trading for Convenience?

Artificial intelligence can make us faster, more productive, and more capable. But those benefits come with costs, risks, and changes that are easy to overlook.

AI is often discussed in extremes: either as a revolutionary technology that will improve everything or as a threat that will make everything worse. Reality is more complicated. AI can be genuinely useful while also creating new problems.

Instead of asking whether AI is simply "good" or "bad," it may be more useful to ask: What are we gaining, what are we giving up, and where is the tradeoff worth it?

So, Is AI Worth It?

AI is not just a tool with a list of advantages and disadvantages. It is a series of tradeoffs.

It can make us faster, more capable, and more productive. But those gains come with questions about money, resources, reliability, privacy, employment, and what we still expect humans to do for themselves.

The question may not be whether AI is worth using.
It may be: Where is the tradeoff worth making?
