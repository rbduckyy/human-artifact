AI: What Are We Trading for Convenience?

AI can save time and expand what people can do—but those benefits come with costs, risks, and changes to human behavior.

AI is often discussed in extremes: either as a revolutionary tool or a dangerous threat. In reality, it is both useful and costly. The important question is not simply whether AI is “good” or “bad,” but what tradeoffs we accept when we use it.

Convenience

Benefit: AI helps people write, summarize, brainstorm, code, and research faster.

Tradeoff: Faster work is not always better work. Speed can reduce reflection and independent thinking.

Question: If AI makes a task easier, what do we stop practicing ourselves?

Final Takeaway

AI is not only changing what we can do. It may also change what we expect humans to do for themselves. The challenge is making sure convenience does not quietly replace judgment, responsibility, and agency.
